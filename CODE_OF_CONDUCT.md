# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Markdown Twin to be a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or language.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Keep discussions on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Insults, personal attacks, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or project work.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit comments. It also applies when representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer [@yukiidayo](https://github.com/yukiidayo) through GitHub. For sensitive reports, use GitHub private reporting where available.

Please do not use public issues to report incidents that involve personal safety or private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. A private warning and clarification of expectations
2. Removal of comments or content
3. A temporary or permanent ban from project spaces

Enforcement decisions will be made in good faith and with respect for the privacy of the people involved.
